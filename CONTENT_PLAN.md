Introduce the decision to join, the Route to the Crypt idea, and the learning question behind it. ## Article 2 — retrospective (write after submission)

Do not fill in the conclusions until the build is complete. Capture the following as they happen:

- What the original idea was and what changed.
- Which Kiro specs, steering files, hooks, or other features were actually used.
- What Kiro did well, where it misunderstood the task, and how the workflow was corrected.
- Which AWS documentation claims were verified, changed, simplified, or removed.
- What was not tested in a live AWS application or environment.
- What went well, what went wrong, and what Maria would change on the next project.
- The working app, public repo, demo, and relevant source links.

The retrospective must report real events and observed results, not planned ones.
